How We Hire Engineers

Our interview process has three stages:

  1. A 45-minute screen with a hiring manager covering your background and motivation.
  2. A take-home project (3-4 hours) building a small service against a spec we provide.
  3. An onsite loop: a system design interview, a code review pairing session, and a conversation about how you mentor and collaborate with junior engineers on your team.

We value engineers who can reason about tradeoffs out loud and who have experience growing less senior teammates.
